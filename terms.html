04Consultation and your health

  • Before chemical, colour, skin or facial treatments, please tell us about any allergies, skin conditions, medication, recent treatments elsewhere, or pregnancy.

  • Our stylists may refuse or change a service if it could damage your hair or skin, or if there is a visible infection or condition that makes it unsafe.
